Fuji Bridge copies photos from your Fujifilm camera to your iPhone, iPad or Mac, quickly and without surprises.

• Import in one tap: the newest 25, the newest 100, or the whole card. Photos you already have are skipped.
• USB or Wi-Fi: plug in the cable for the fastest copy (over 20 MB/s measured with an X100VI), or go wireless.
• Wakes the camera over Bluetooth: once paired, Fuji Bridge asks the camera to turn on its Wi-Fi and offers to join it.
• Preview before importing: browse the card, pick the frames you want, and look at any of them at full size.
• Resumes where it stopped: a dropped connection picks up from the last byte instead of starting the file over.
• A calm library: a justified grid that makes room for the pictures, and a full-screen viewer with swipe, zoom and keyboard navigation on Mac.
• Diagnostics you can read: every import keeps a report with the time each step took, the speed of each file, and what went wrong if something did. Share it in one tap.

Your photos stay on your device: in Files › Fuji Bridge on iPhone and iPad, and in Pictures › Fuji Bridge on Mac. No account, no tracking, no data collected.

Tested with the Fujifilm X100VI. Other recent Fujifilm cameras with USB or smartphone Wi-Fi transfer may work too.
