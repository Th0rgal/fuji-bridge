Fuji Bridge imports photos from a Fujifilm camera (tested with the X100VI) over USB, or over the camera's own Wi-Fi network, which the app can ask the camera to turn on through Bluetooth.

The app needs a Fujifilm camera to import anything; it has no account and no sign-in. Without a camera, you can still open the Imported tab, the photo viewer, and Diagnostics (stethoscope button), and the camera card explains how to connect.

To test with a camera:
1. USB: plug the camera into the iPhone, iPad or Mac with a USB-C cable, then tap Import.
2. Wireless: tap the Bluetooth button on the camera card, pair from the camera (Bluetooth › Pairing Registration), then tap Import. The camera starts its Wi-Fi and iOS asks to join its network.

Permissions: Bluetooth (find the camera and wake its Wi-Fi), Local Network (talk to the camera at 192.168.0.1 over Wi-Fi), Hotspot Configuration (join the camera's network on request). On Mac the app is sandboxed and saves to Pictures › Fuji Bridge.

The screenshots and previews show real captures of the app: imports run against a staged card with the same protocol as the camera.
